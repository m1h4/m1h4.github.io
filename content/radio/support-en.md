## Play a station

Open Stations and tap a station to listen. The mini-player shows what is playing; tap it or swipe up to open Now Playing. Radio is live: Stop disconnects the stream and Play rejoins the live broadcast, so there is no pausing or rewinding. Audio continues in the background, with controls on the Lock Screen and in Control Center.

## Favorites

Touch and hold a station and choose Add to favorites, or tap the star in Now Playing. Favorites appear at the top of Stations and in the Favorites tab, where Edit lets you reorder or remove them. When you are signed in to iCloud, favorites sync between your devices.

## Saved Songs

To keep a song you hear, tap ••• in Now Playing and choose Save Song, or touch and hold a song under Recent songs. Save Song is available when the station provides song information. Find your songs under Favorites → Saved Songs. There you can search and remove them, or open a song to search for it in Apple Music or share it. When you are signed in to iCloud, saved songs sync between your devices; otherwise they stay on the device and sync once you sign in.

## Sleep timer

While a station is playing, tap ••• in Now Playing, choose Sleep Timer, and pick 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. The countdown appears below the playback controls, next to Cancel timer. When it ends, Radio stops the stream. Changing stations keeps the timer running; stopping playback yourself cancels it.

## Search and filters

Search finds stations by name, city, region, or type of programming. Before you type, you can browse by programming or by region, or repeat a recent search. In Stations, the filter button in the toolbar narrows the list by Collection and Region; choose Clear filters to see every station again.

## Clear your history

Recently Played lists the stations you listened to last. Choose See All, then swipe left on a station to remove it or tap Clear History. In Search, swipe left on a recent search to remove it, or tap Clear All. Neither list syncs to iCloud.

## A station won’t play?

Streams are run by the broadcasters and can be temporarily unavailable. Check your internet connection, try another station, and try again later. If the connection drops while you listen, Radio shows Waiting for connection and reconnects by itself when the connection returns, for up to five minutes; after that, tap Play. You can also tap Try again while it waits. While Radio is in the background, iOS may delay reconnecting until you open the app. If a station stays unavailable, let us know as described below.

## Missing or wrong song information?

Song titles, artists, and cover art come from each broadcaster, through its stream or its own song feed. Not every station provides them, and what is provided can be late, incomplete, or wrong. Radio shows it as supplied and does not identify songs itself. When there is no current song, Radio shows the station’s name and artwork instead. For a station without a recent-songs list, the Recent songs section offers a link to the station’s website.

## AirPlay and speakers

In Now Playing, tap the output name below the volume slider to choose an AirPlay speaker or another audio output. The mini-player also shows an AirPlay button when there is room. If the selected output disconnects, Radio stops; tap Play to continue.

## Request or report a station

To suggest a new station or report one that no longer plays, email [support@mihasoftware.com](mailto:support@mihasoftware.com) and name the station. Changes to the station list arrive with app updates.

## Requirements

Radio needs an iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later, and an internet connection for listening. The app is available in English and Croatian.

## Get in touch

Email [support@mihasoftware.com](mailto:support@mihasoftware.com) with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the Radio version shown in About, the station’s name, and a description of the problem.
