This policy explains how Radio: Croatian Stations (“Radio”), provided by Miha Software, handles information.

## No accounts, advertising, or tracking

Radio is a free app for iPhone and iPad that plays live streams of Croatian radio stations. It has no accounts, no advertising SDKs, and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and Miha Software runs no server for it. We receive no information about what you listen to. Radio asks for no system permissions: it does not request access to your location, microphone, or contacts, and it does not ask to track you.

## On your device

Radio stores your favorite stations, up to 20 recently played stations, up to ten recent searches, the last station you selected, and the songs you save. It also caches the recent-songs lists of stations you have played or opened for up to 24 hours. Radio does not send any of this to us. You can remove favorites, recently played stations, recent searches, and saved songs in the app, and clear the station history or the recent searches all at once. Deleting Radio removes what it stored on the device.

## iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Radio syncs two things between your devices. The ordered list of your favorite stations syncs through iCloud key-value storage. Saved Songs sync through your private iCloud database (CloudKit). For each saved song, the title, artist, station, and save date are stored there in an encrypted field, along with the album, artwork link, and broadcast time when the broadcaster supplies them. This information lives in your own iCloud account under Apple’s terms; Miha Software cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, favorites and saved songs stay only on your device.

Removing a favorite or a saved song in the app also removes it from iCloud and from your other devices. For a removed song, iCloud keeps only a small marker without the song’s details, so that another device cannot bring it back. Deleting the app does not remove what is already in your iCloud account.

iCloud sends Radio silent background notifications, which are used only to keep Saved Songs in sync between your devices. Radio shows no notifications and asks for no notification permission.

## Connections to broadcasters

Playing a station connects your device directly to that broadcaster’s streaming provider. To show what is playing and a station’s recent songs, Radio also connects to the public song-information feed that the broadcaster or its service provider publishes, for the station you are playing and for any station whose recent songs you open. Cover art, when a broadcaster supplies it, may load from an image host chosen by that broadcaster. These parties receive your IP address and standard request information, and handle it under their own policies. Radio performs no song recognition and no external artwork search.

## External links and sharing

Station websites open in your browser. Search in Apple Music opens Apple Music or your browser with the saved song’s title and artist as the search text. Sharing a saved song uses the system share sheet, and the song goes only where you send it. Those apps and websites handle information under their own policies.

## Support

If you email support@mihasoftware.com, we receive the information you choose to include. It is used to respond and investigate your request, not for advertising. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for the request and applicable record-keeping obligations; contact us to request deletion.

## Platform diagnostics

Apple may provide developers with aggregate crash and usage information from people who have chosen to share it, under Apple’s settings and policies. Radio adds no third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact [support@mihasoftware.com](mailto:support@mihasoftware.com). This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
