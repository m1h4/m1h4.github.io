Photo Resize processes photos on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network permission. The developer does not collect your photos or other personal data.

Only the photo you choose or share with the app is accessed. Original files are never modified. Resized copies omit location and other source photo metadata; sharing Original preserves the original file and metadata.

Temporary copies stay in the app’s private cache. On subsequent launches, copies unused for 24 hours are removed. Android may clear the cache earlier. Copies are excluded from app backup.

When you tap a size and choose another app in Android’s sharing menu, that app receives temporary access to the selected file. Its handling of the photo is governed by its own privacy practices.

A system photo provider may download a cloud photo before giving it to Photo Resize. Photo Resize itself does not upload or download photos.
