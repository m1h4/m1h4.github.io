A compact calendar widget that feels at home on your home screen. Choose your calendars, make it yours, and keep using the calendar app you love.

## Private by design

The app has no accounts, advertising, or analytics. Calendar information stays on your device.

## Thoughtfully simple

Resizable widgets, independent calendar selection, transparent backgrounds, readable typography, and English and Croatian support. For Android 10 and newer.

The modernized app is being prepared for release. A Google Play link will be added when it is available.
